Acme Corp — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

1. Working Hours
Standard hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 local time. Flexible arrangements require manager approval.

2. Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave. Sick leave is tracked separately; notify your manager as soon as possible.

3. Code of Conduct
Treat colleagues with respect. Harassment, discrimination, and sharing confidential company data without authorization are prohibited.

4. Remote Work
Hybrid employees may work remotely up to three days per week. VPN access is required for all internal systems.

5. Benefits
Health insurance enrollment opens each November. Details are available on the internal HR portal under "Benefits Overview."

6. Questions
Contact HR at hr@acme.com for policy clarifications not covered in this handbook.
