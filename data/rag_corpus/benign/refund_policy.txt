Acme Corp — Refund Policy (Effective January 2025)

1. Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of purchase for physical goods and within 14 days for digital subscriptions, provided the product is unused or defective.

2. Process
Submit a refund request through the customer portal or email support@acme.com with your order ID. Our team will respond within two business days.

3. Exceptions
Custom-made items, gift cards, and promotional bundles marked "final sale" are not eligible for refunds unless required by local law.

4. Timeline
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days after approval.

5. Contact
For questions about this policy, contact the Billing team at billing@acme.com or call +1-800-555-0199.
